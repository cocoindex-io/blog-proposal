# Blog Proposal

This is an open space where you can propose blog posts on CocoIndex :hugs:.

## Examples
You can use the following examples as a reference for how to structure your proposal.

- [Rust Ownership](rust-ownership/) by CocoIndex maintainer
- [Extraction DSPy](extraction-dspy/) by our community member Dhilip

## How to Contribute
1. Create an issue to open a topic for discussion before writing or discuss with our team in our [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/zpA9S2Dr7s) (optional)
2. Create a subfolder for your blog and follow the structure of the examples above.
3. Send a PR with your proposal.

## Quality
Our team pays great attention to the quality of the blogs that we share with our community. You can review our [past blogs](https://cocoindex.io/blogs/) for reference on style and depth.


## Publication
Publication will depend on the quality and relevance of the content after review by our team. We appreciate every contribution and will do our best to provide feedback.

Please feel free to contribute, and contact us in our [Discord server](https://discord.com/invite/zpA9S2Dr7s) if you have any questions.
